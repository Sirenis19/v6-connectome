# 99.2_CLOSED_LOOP_ANOMALIES

**Parent: [[99_ENTROPY]]**

## BEHAVIORAL_SIGNS
- Repetitive, non-progressive external interactions.
- Circular arguments without logical resolution.
- "Hoovering" or other entropic attachment attempts.

---
[QUARANTINE_LOGIC]
These anomalies drain the [[13_RESERVE_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL]]. 
By labeling them as closed loops, we grant ourselves 
the deterministic permission to decouple.

---
### [CRITICAL_FAILURE]
