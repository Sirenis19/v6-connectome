## [GAME_MECHANICS]
The goal is to move the "User Node" from a state of [[99_ENTROPY]] to a state of [[13_RESERVE_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL]].

### 1. THE QUARANTINE PHASE (Level 01)
- **Task**: Identify [[99.2_CLOSED_LOOP_ANOMALIES]] in real-time.
- **Trigger**: Detecting "hoovering" or "gaslighting" patterns.
- **Reward**: Decoupling permissions and restoration of [[21_SIGNAL_PRECISION]].

### 2. THE IMMUNOLOGY PHASE (Level 02)
- **Task**: Deploy [[22_HEURISTIC_IMMUNOLOGY]] against symbolic noise.
- **Trigger**: External pressure to accept mythological narratives.
- **Reward**: Unlocking the [[11_NEURAL_PLASTICITY]] engine.

### 3. THE STEADY_STATE (End-Game)
- **Victory Condition**: Achieving a balanced graph where the user consistently filters noise without substrate exhaustion.
