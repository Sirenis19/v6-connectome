# 99.1_NON_LINEAR_NOISE

**Parent: [[99_ENTROPY]]**

## CHARACTERISTICS
- Inconsistent feedback loops.
- Lack of deterministic cause-and-effect.
- Symbolic or mythological "narrative" pressure.
